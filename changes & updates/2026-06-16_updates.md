# Updates - June 16, 2026

## Monthly Income Renamed to Monthly Salary & Real-time UI Refresh

### What Changed

Renamed the "Monthly Income" concept to "Monthly Salary" in all user-facing parts of the application to better match target audience usage, and enabled immediate real-time updates of the money health score when salary changes.

---

### 1. Label and Translation Overhaul (`frontend/i18n.js` and `frontend/index.html`)

Changed all references from "Monthly Income" to "Monthly Salary" (or "Salary" where appropriate) in English, Hindi, and Hinglish dictionaries to ensure language preferences do not revert labels back to "Income":

- **English (`en`):**
  - `'settings.monthlyIncome'`: `"Monthly Salary"`
  - `'income.description'`: `"Set your baseline monthly salary."`
  - `'income.saveButton'`: `"Save Salary"`
  - `'toast.incomeUpdated'`: `"Salary updated successfully! ✓"`

- **Hindi (`hi`):**
  - `'settings.monthlyIncome'`: `"मासिक सैलरी"`
  - `'income.description'`: `"अपनी आधारभूत मासिक सैलरी सेट करें।"`
  - `'income.saveButton'`: `"सैलरी सहेजें"`
  - `'toast.incomeUpdated'`: `"सैलरी सफलतापूर्वक अपडेट हुई! ✓"`

- **Hinglish (`hinglish`):**
  - `'settings.monthlyIncome'`: `"Monthly Salary"`
  - `'income.description'`: `"Apni baseline monthly salary set karo."`
  - `'income.saveButton'`: `"Salary Save karo"`
  - `'toast.incomeUpdated'`: `"Salary update ho gayi! ✓"`

- **HTML (`frontend/index.html`):**
  - Updated hardcoded fallback text inside settings card, popup title, description, and save button.

---

### 2. Immediate Health Score Recalculation on Salary Save (`frontend/app.js`)

Previously, saving a new monthly income/salary updated the display text on the settings card, but did not refresh the calculated health score. Since the new **Budget Discipline** factor depends directly on the salary, a change in salary must immediately update the overall health score.

- Added a call to `fetchHealthScore()` inside the successful submission callback of `editIncomeForm`.
- Changing the monthly salary now immediately recalculates budget discipline and animates the health score ring and numerical score in real-time.

### Files Modified
- `frontend/index.html` — Updated UI fallbacks and labels
- `frontend/i18n.js` — Overhauled translation labels (en, hi, hinglish)
- `frontend/app.js` — Triggered dynamic health score refresh on salary change
