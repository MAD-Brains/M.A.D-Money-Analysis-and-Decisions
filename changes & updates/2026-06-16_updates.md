# Updates - June 16, 2026

## Monthly Income Renamed to Monthly Salary & Real-time UI Refresh

### What Changed

Renamed the "Monthly Income" concept to "Monthly Salary" in all user-facing parts of the application to better match target audience usage, and enabled immediate real-time updates of the money health score when salary changes.

---

### 1. Label and Translation Overhaul (`frontend/i18n.js` and `frontend/index.html`)

Changed all references from "Monthly Income" to "Monthly Salary" (or "Salary" where appropriate) in English, Hindi, and Hinglish dictionaries to ensure language preferences do not revert labels back to "Income":

- **English (`en`):**
  - `'settings.monthlyIncome'`: `"Monthly Salary"`
  - `'income.description'`: `"Set your baseline monthly salary."`
  - `'income.saveButton'`: `"Save Salary"`
  - `'toast.incomeUpdated'`: `"Salary updated successfully! ✓"`

- **Hindi (`hi`):**
  - `'settings.monthlyIncome'`: `"मासिक सैलरी"`
  - `'income.description'`: `"अपनी आधारभूत मासिक सैलरी सेट करें।"`
  - `'income.saveButton'`: `"सैलरी सहेजें"`
  - `'toast.incomeUpdated'`: `"सैलरी सफलतापूर्वक अपडेट हुई! ✓"`

- **Hinglish (`hinglish`):**
  - `'settings.monthlyIncome'`: `"Monthly Salary"`
  - `'income.description'`: `"Apni baseline monthly salary set karo."`
  - `'income.saveButton'`: `"Salary Save karo"`
  - `'toast.incomeUpdated'`: `"Salary update ho gayi! ✓"`

- **HTML (`frontend/index.html`):**
  - Updated hardcoded fallback text inside settings card, popup title, description, and save button.

---

### 2. Immediate Health Score Recalculation on Salary Save (`frontend/app.js`)

Previously, saving a new monthly income/salary updated the display text on the settings card, but did not refresh the calculated health score. Since the new **Budget Discipline** factor depends directly on the salary, a change in salary must immediately update the overall health score.

- Added a call to `fetchHealthScore()` inside the successful submission callback of `editIncomeForm`.
- Changing the monthly salary now immediately recalculates budget discipline and animates the health score ring and numerical score in real-time.

### Files Modified
- `frontend/index.html` — Updated UI fallbacks and labels
- `frontend/i18n.js` — Overhauled translation labels (en, hi, hinglish)
- `frontend/app.js` — Triggered dynamic health score refresh on salary change

## Fix: Monthly Salary Now Auto-Populates Insights & Affects Health Score

### What Changed

The rename above documented a salary-aware **Budget Discipline** factor, but the actual calculation logic for that factor — and any use of `monthlyIncome` in Insights/health score — had been stripped out during the rename. As a result, setting a Monthly Salary in the profile had no effect on the Insights overview or the Money Health Score; it only updated the settings display text. This fix restores and wires up the salary-aware behavior end-to-end.

---

### 1. Salary Auto-Populates Income (`backend/services/healthScore.js`, `backend/routes/insights.js`)

Both `calculateHealthScore()` and `GET /api/insights/overview` now compute:

```
effectiveIncome = max(income logged this month, profile Monthly Salary)
```

- If the user hasn't logged any income transaction yet this month, the profile's Monthly Salary is used as the baseline income for Savings, Savings Rate, and the "Wants % of income" Jarvis check.
- If logged income (e.g. bonus/freelance) exceeds the salary, the higher actual figure is used instead — no double counting.

### 2. Budget Discipline Factor Restored (`backend/services/healthScore.js`)

- Added back a 5th scoring factor — **Budget Discipline** — based on the `monthly expense / Monthly Salary` ratio (only active when salary > 0 is set in profile).
- When a salary is set, factor weights are redistributed:
  - Savings Rate: 35% → 30%
  - Spending Diversity: 20% → 15%
  - Consistency: 25% → 20%
  - Expense Control: 20% → 15%
  - Budget Discipline (new): 20%
- When no salary is set, weights stay exactly as before (35/20/25/20) — fully backward compatible.
- Added new Hinglish subtitle tips for the Budget Discipline factor when it's the weakest area.

### 3. New Jarvis Advice Tip (`backend/routes/insights.js`)

Added a "Budget" category warning shown when the Budget Discipline score < 50:

> "Salary ke against kharcha zyada ho raha hai! Budget tight karo warna month end me dikkat hogi 💳"

### 4. Insights Refresh on Salary Save (`frontend/app.js`)

Saving a new Monthly Salary now also calls `fetchInsightsOverview()` (in addition to the existing `fetchHealthScore()`), so the Insights page numbers (Income, Savings, Savings Rate) update immediately too.

### Files Modified
- `backend/services/healthScore.js` — Salary auto-populates income; restored Budget Discipline factor with weight redistribution
- `backend/routes/insights.js` — Salary auto-populates Insights income/savings/savings-rate; added Budget Discipline Jarvis tip
- `frontend/app.js` — Refresh Insights overview on salary save
